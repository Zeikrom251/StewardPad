The first Alpha release of StewardPad for Windows: race control for Le Mans Ultimate stewards, on
your own PC.

### Added

- **Live race control.** Standings for every car from Le Mans Ultimate, or a simulator to
  train and demo without the game.
- **Log it now, review it later.** Press Space and the incident is stamped a few seconds back
  (10 by default), where the replay needs to start. Pick the cars from the standings or with
  1 to 9.
- **The incident inspector.** Status in one click, every car with its part in it, an
  investigation written for drivers and steward notes that stay between stewards.
- **Review queue.** Every undecided incident in race order, one at a time, full screen.
- **Your league's rule book.** Import it from Google Docs or Word, keep its numbering
  (3.3.a, 3.4.b.iii), cite the rules an incident broke and edit the book in the app.
- **Publishing.** A formal stewards' decisions document (web page or PDF), a penalty sheet,
  driver and full-log spreadsheets and a results JSON file.
- **Live Discord announcements.** Each status change posted to your league's channel, with
  the embed you design.
- **Several stewards, one session.** Share session files and merge them.
