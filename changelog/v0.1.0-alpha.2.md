The second Alpha release of StewardPad for Windows.

### Added

- **Delete several incidents at once.** On the Incidents page, tick the incidents you want
  to remove and click **Delete** in the selection bar, next to Set status and Merge. StewardPad
  asks once more ("Delete 2 incidents?") before anything is removed, and the selected
  incidents go in one step: if one of them no longer exists, none is deleted.
- **Fold the Race page's side panel.** The arrow at the top right of the Quick log folds the
  quick log and the incident list into a thin strip, so the standings get the whole width. The
  strip keeps the **Log incident** button (and **Space** still logs); the arrow on it brings the
  panel back. StewardPad remembers your choice the next time you open it.

### Fixed

- **The cars' other incidents stay inside the Cars section.** In the incident panel and on the
  Review page, the "Also in" list under each car could run past the section and over the rest
  of the panel. It now wraps onto more lines, and past ten incidents the rest fold behind a
  "+N more" link that shows them all.
- **Settings → Updates says why a check failed.** It used to answer "Could not reach GitHub"
  to every problem. It now tells apart no connection, no release published yet, and a build
  for a system StewardPad has no releases for.
